**Collaborative Research: CPS: Medium: Enabling Autonomous, Persistent, and Adaptive Mobile Observational Networks Through Energy-Aware Dynamic Coverage** ([NSF Award #2223845](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/show-award?AWD_ID=2223845))

Long-duration measurements for weather, climate, and surveillance call for mobile observational networks that can operate persistently with little human intervention. This project develops and validates methods for managing networks made up of a renewably powered *host* agent and smaller *satellite* agents that the host deploys and recharges. Energy is treated as renewable but limited in instantaneous power, so the network has to continuously balance energy harvesting against information gathering.

The approach combines:

- a **dynamic coverage model** that captures how information about the environment decays and is regained (clarity),
- a **statistical energy model** of harvesting and consumption,
- a **multi-level predictive controller**, in which the host uses an *information value of energy* to decide when to explore, recharge, or redeploy satellites.

The methods are validated on UAV networks, on combined solar-powered surface vessel and UAV networks, and in simulation on networks that add undersea gliders. The project also supports internships and K-12 outreach.
